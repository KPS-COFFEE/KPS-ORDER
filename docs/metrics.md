# KPS ORDER metric and closure definitions

All amounts are integer Saudi halalas. Operational dates use `Asia/Riyadh` and the configured `operational_day_start` (HH:MM), independently of branch order acceptance hours. If unconfigured, the API explicitly marks `operational_day_configured=false` and warns that dates are calendar days; no 05:30 operation assumption is made. Dashboard order intake cohorts use `[created_at start, created_at end)`. Completed sales, completed counts and execution-branch/item sales use `[completed_at start, completed_at end)`. Explicit range is limited to 93 days. Access is restricted to permitted brand/branch before aggregation.

- New: preparation state `new`. Active: unfinished and not new/cancelled/rejected. Completed: a real `completed_at` recorded by the authorized fulfillment transition. Cancelled/rejected orders are never completed sales.
- Gross completed amount: each completed order total once, including its snapshotted delivery fee. Net completed amount deducts ordinary refunds and approved price credits no earlier than completion: a prior adjustment is recognized with the sale, and a later adjustment in its own reporting period. Returning a prepayment for an uncompleted cancelled order changes collections/refunds only, never completed sales. Amendment excess-payment refunds do not deduct an already reduced sale again. Actual cash/recorded-card movements retain their own dates. Approved closure/month snapshots never change.
- Top items: original quantities of orders completed within the period, less explicitly refunded line quantities known before the period end. Unattributed financial refunds have no guessed quantity effect. Net item amount allocates financial order refunds proportionally to each original line amount, excluding fee from line rows. Item net excludes delivery and original discounts before deterministic integer allocation. Later adjustments use the existing proportional monetary projection over original products; recorded returned quantities remain separate. Allocated product amounts conserve the projected item net exactly. These are completion-cohort item values as of the period end, not a second cash total. Top branches use the same completed-sales/refund/credit ledger as the summary and attribute each order to its actual fulfillment branch once; the response lists up to 20 branches.
- Delivery time: authorized branch pickup timestamp to successful delivery timestamp. Average includes only nonnegative intervals with both recorded timestamps; valid and excluded counts are returned. V5 classifications use the policy version frozen on each order; missing timestamps never imply on-time service.
- Actual collections use collection-event timestamps for the selected period. Cash is the order amount after change, never the tendered banknote amount. Card requires a real reference. Custody handovers never enter sales or collection totals.
- Driver expected custody = approved opening balance + net cash collections + confirmed incoming transfers + authorized cash receipts/adjustments − cash refunds/approved expenses − prior confirmed outgoing transfers. Card transactions do not enter physical cash custody.
- Final submission freezes expected, counted, proposed final handover, retained balance and discrepancy. Counted must equal proposed handover plus retained. Final handover is a separate pending transfer; actual recipient must confirm, including a zero-amount acknowledgment. Approval requires that confirmation. Only the owner can approve a visible discrepancy or retained custody, with a reason. Approved discrepancy creates a linked immutable adjustment while preserving the submitted expected/count difference. Retained custody is persisted as a linked carryover, automatically included in the same user’s next shift opening, never as a new sale.
- Manager expected custody derives only from its own custody ledger, including confirmed driver handovers and advances. Manager summary collections come from distinct linked collection IDs, not transfer events. Driver shifts without tasks must close before the manager. Partial handovers count only when confirmed. Submitted/closed shifts accept no new financial events, except the final submitted handover confirmation.
- Reports store schema/version, operational date, timestamps/timezone, preparer/approver, distinct order/task IDs, collection/refund records, custody events/transfers and child closure references. Snapshot update/delete and ledger update/delete are prohibited by PostgreSQL triggers. PDF/CSV are persisted BYTEA; a generation failure leaves closure closed and exposes failed status with retry.

No live GPS, external notification delivery, online payment, tax invoice issuance or treasury integration is claimed. Driver handover of a collected task is blocked: the original collector retains custody responsibility; uncollected task transfer requires the receiving driver to accept explicitly.

Closed-report correction uses owner-authorized `/api/shifts/{open_shift_id}/cash-event` with `corrects_report_id`. It inserts an immutable correction link and monotonically numbered correction version, while the original snapshot/file remain intact. Original report detail includes correction records and the resulting later closure-report reference once that shift closes. Responsibility handover between managers requires an actual acceptance API by the incoming manager; it transfers active driver responsibility, with cash separately handed over and confirmed.

`summary.live_overdue` counts unfinished delivery orders older than the legacy order-age alert threshold since order creation, excluding cancellations/rejections. Its unconfigured 60-minute UI fallback is marked `threshold_configured=false`, not a delivery promise. Canonical `delivery_time.delayed` and `delivery_performance` instead use each order's frozen province policy, matching order lists and saved closure reports. Unconfigured policies stay neutral. `legacy_delayed` remains for compatibility only and is not the V5 road-performance classification.

All operations/chat mutation transactions take a per-brand PostgreSQL advisory lock before their specific row locks. This deliberately serializes the small emergency site's financial mutation lane to avoid inconsistent multi-record locking during task, custody, and manager handovers. Reads and different brands remain independent. Replay of a previously successful idempotency key rechecks current resource authorization before disclosing its cached response; withdrawing branch/brand access does not preserve old access via a saved key.

## V5 province standards and identity

Preparation duration runs from branch acceptance to branch readiness. Driver wait runs from readiness to driver pickup. Delivery duration retains the V4 pickup-to-delivery definition. Total duration runs from order confirmation to completion. A phase that has not begun has no elapsed value; missing/cancelled intervals are excluded rather than counted as zero.

Province standards have immutable versions, effective timestamps, and explicit `delivery` or `total` basis. T1 and T2 are positive seconds, T1<T2; excellent is <=T1, good is >T1 and <=T2, late is >T2. No configured policy is neutral. Confirmation captures the actual effective policy; future changes do not recolor historical approved snapshots. Live order age remains a separate alert.

Human order numbers use a PostgreSQL sequence, never MAX()+1. A restore advances that sequence past the restored maximum. Cancellation never renumbers an order.

V5 compact and owner report renderings reference the same immutable saved closure snapshot with a source digest and template version. Original approved V4 files remain available. New presentation files are stored separately and protected once ready. Owner notifications and encrypted mail outbox are durable and deduplicated; provider acceptance is distinct from delivery. Email or report failure does not repeat cash or reopen the shift.

Guest phone/email contact is explicitly unverified. Guest ownership is a server session principal, independent of a typed phone, sequential reference, URL, or caller-supplied customer ID. Email verification establishes only email ownership. Staff invitations fix role/scope on the server and keep pending accounts inactive until proof.

## Discounted item-return allocation

The discounted item net excludes delivery and free gifts. Integer halala budgets are allocated cumulatively in immutable snapshot order, and quantity returns consume their product budget cumulatively. Returning all paid item quantities conserves the full discounted item net; request ordering cannot change the entitlement. Actual refund availability and documented-prior-return checks remain authoritative; new item effects are additionally capped by actual previously posted refunds and approved physical credits, so a legacy rounding convention cannot consume delivery fees. Retrospective labeling of validated existing refund rows may use their original conservative floor allocation, without posting money. This allocation neither posts money nor rewrites past refunds or approved physical-return credits.

## Accounting delivery-day allocation — owner decision 29 October 2026

The accounting delivery report uses the owning manager duty's reporting operational date for order counts, completed sales and customer receipts. Morning and evening duties labeled with the same date aggregate together. A receipt at 04:15 or 04:27 remains with its owning duty, even after the clock crosses the next day boundary. Manager date takes precedence over a driver's mismatched date; effective task attribution wins after an approved transfer or attribution correction. Accepted, unassigned orders use their recorded accepting manager duty, falling back to the recorded creation duty. No clock-based inference creates a manager association.

Refunds follow their processing duty when present; a later-duty refund does not move the original receipt. Without a processing duty, the refund retains its actual transaction period. Later invoice price credits retain their documented approval period. Non-delivery records without a delivery duty keep their transaction-time policy. All amounts remain integer halalas, and all actual creation/completion/collection/refund timestamps, approved closure snapshots and frozen month snapshots remain unchanged. The JSON report, screen, PDF and Excel share the same allocation and column contract.

Regression examples: KFJ11-261027-0140 (11 SAR collected 28 October 04:15) and HFR2-261027-0367 (20 SAR collected 28 October 04:27) belong to duty day 27. QSM10-261028-0120 (16.50 SAR collected 29 October 04:19) belongs to duty day 28. The expected live day-28 figure from the inspected records is 2,546 SAR, not the incomplete 2,529.50 SAR subtotal: 2,560.50 - 31 + 16.50.

Accounting manager cards include every authorized manager's operationally finished duty, even while financial allocation is pending and no final manager/driver snapshot exists. These cards show manager, reporting date, morning/evening period, reference and pending child-record count. Missing evidence remains pending with 0% progress, never a fabricated zero-value financial approval. Existing final manager reports remain printable. Independent review and all final-day financial approval gates remain enforced.
