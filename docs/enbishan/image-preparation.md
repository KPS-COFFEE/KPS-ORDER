Recovered from private preview source commit `71cb1970656747439a56c08e43470f3510e53b5a` without changing or republishing the Site.

The original owner logo is preserved at `public/assets/enbishan-original.jpeg`. Forty-three source-linked menu images are bundled under `public/assets/enbishan-menu/`; eleven have the free-delivery slogan and old phone removed, as approved. The additional eleven workbook source images were recovered on 2026-10-20; six are byte-identical originals and five were edited through the built-in image tool, then visually checked for removal of the claim/phone and preservation of product imagery, titles, prices and logos. Original downloads remain separate from edited copies.

`app/enbishan_menu_assets.json` maps each exact original workbook reference to its approved local asset and SHA-256. The draft importer resolves only this immutable allowlist or brand-owned uploaded product assets; arbitrary URLs and paths are never fetched. Every cover and variant upload is checked for Enbishan asset ownership when applying. Bundled paths are verified against their hashes.

The same customer controls show the cover and switch to the first unit’s selected variant photo. Product prices and drafts remain unchanged by artwork binding. No missing product photos have been fabricated: six drinks and Ramadan source row 56 have no supplied image URL.

Source-artwork discrepancies remain explicit: source row 41 artwork prints 11 pieces, while the approved order configuration is 10 (5 crispy + 5 cabbage). This cleanup retains the supplied numeral; it does not authorize changing the menu quantity. Source rows 52–54 share one grape-leaf/mojito image although their plate choices differ. These are source-image limitations for owner review, not invented replacement photos.
